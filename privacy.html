Privacy Policy

Personal Rclone is a private integration used to access the owner's Google Drive account from a Linux computer.

Google Drive data is accessed only to browse, read, create, modify, and manage files at the owner's request.

Authentication credentials are stored locally. File content may be temporarily cached locally to support reading and writing.

This integration does not operate a separate server that collects or shares Google Drive data. Google Drive data is not sold to third parties.

Access can be revoked at any time through Google Account settings.
